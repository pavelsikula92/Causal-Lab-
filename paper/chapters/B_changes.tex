\section{Relation to the first version}\label{app:changes}

The first published version of this programme~\cite{Sikula2026v1} stated it in a broad form. This
appendix relates the two: what is kept, what is reformulated, and what is no longer claimed. Every change
results from a test or a derivation in the present work.

\subsection{Kept}

\begin{itemize}
\item The primacy of causality over geometry; events without intrinsic properties; the causal order as
  the deepest structure.
\item Causal continuity: influence is conserved at every event (for a field of the order, as the Noether charge of its action; Axiom~\ref{ax:continuity}).
\item Finite capacity, now as a bound on the flow through an event.
\item The local response variables $\Pi_v$, $I_v$ and $Z_v=I_v/\Pi_v$, and the Causal Resistance
  Functional $\mathcal R=\sum_vZ_v\Delta\tau$, now as a functional of resistance whose relation to the action of
  a flow is open.
\item The picture of $c$ as the maximal congestion-free throughput and of $c_{\rm eff}$ falling where the
  substrate is loaded. It is now the throughput principle, which gives $\gamma=1$ in three spatial
  dimensions.
\item The separation of the causal, bridge and observational layers.
\item The ontological themes: observation and causal resolution, branching and merging, horizons as
  limits of reconstructability, superposition as coexisting continuations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Reformulated}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The substrate.} A graph with bounded degree $K_0$, locality by graph distance and time as a
  count of updates has a rest frame. It is replaced by a Lorentz-invariant causal set, as required by the
  theorem of Bombelli, Henson and Sorkin and supported by the tests F0.3, F1.8, F6.1 and F6.2.
\item \textbf{Dimension.} The effective dimension is no longer defined by the growth of graph balls; it is
  read from the order (fraction of related pairs, blind instruments).
\item \textbf{The dynamical principle.} The action of a flow is its proper time counted by the order,
  $S_m=-\sum m\,\Delta\tau$; the Causal Resistance Functional is not identified with it, because no relation between
  the impedance and the mass has been derived. The dynamics of the order is the Benincasa--Dowker action.
\item \textbf{Gravity.} The inverse-square law from flux through spherical shells is replaced by the
  response of the order: clocks, cones and density, with the tensor coupling derived and tested.
\item \textbf{Special relativity.} The Lorentz factor is no longer derived from clocks at rest relative to
  the substrate; Lorentz invariance is a property of the substrate.
\item \textbf{Gravity changes the causal order.} Near matter the cones narrow and events are added;
  causality itself (no loops, conservation of flow) is untouched.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{No longer claimed}

\begin{itemize}
\item The cosmological anchor $a_0=cH_0/2\pi$ and the low-acceleration regime built on it, including the
  reinterpretation of dark matter. The anchor is not derived from the present formulation, and its
  mechanism relied on the bounded-degree substrate.
\item The plateau $D_{\rm eff}\approx3$ as an attractor of a bounded-degree dynamics.
\item Threshold effects in strong gravity (suppression of the redshift of white dwarfs, environment-dependent
  delays of gravitational waves), and strong-field properties of a scalar field equation. Beyond the
  linear order the theory is completed by general relativity.
\item The projection of time and length through the Hubble scale and the projection of impedance through the
  vacuum electromagnetic impedance.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Changes since the previous version}

This version adds the results the laboratory obtained after the previous version of this paper. Nothing earlier
is withdrawn; the statuses that changed are marked in the text.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Matter as a defect of a field of the order} (Sec.~\ref{sec:fielddefect}): identity, Lorentz
  contraction and inertia equal to rest energy in 1+1 and 2+1 (F3.14b, F3.15), swerves with no free constant
  (F3.14c), fall in 1+1 and on a curved 2+1 order (F5.2a3, F5.2b, F5.2c2), the active side in its scalar branch
  (F5.2d), and the 3+1 Green function of the order (F3.16b, F5.3).
\item \textbf{Varying the order} (Sec.~\ref{sec:variation}): the vacuum control (F6.9a); the source of the order from a
  matter action that depends on the order alone (F6.10a, F6.10b); the conservation of its energy as a Noether charge
  (development), which replaces a crossing-pair energy whose expectation is zero; the young-field stiffness in all
  three sectors (F6.8g); and the linear field equation of the order with its spin-0 form factor, the field of dust
  at finite $k\ell_k$ and the repulsion of a static domain wall (derived from the frozen records).
\item \textbf{The selection principle}: the action alone does not hold the flat order under local moves
  (development); the field equation is taken as the saddle of the ensemble mean at linear order and as the balance of
  action and entropy, Eq.~\eqref{eq:gammabeta}, beyond it.
\item \textbf{Statuses}: causal continuity holds for a field as a Noether charge, not as the retarded influence; the
  action is extended to fields; the summary of Part~\ref{part:axioms}, the ontology, the predictions (swerves, a
  falsification condition for the wall) and the discussion are updated accordingly.
\item \textbf{Figures}: three new figures, for matter as a field defect (Fig.~\ref{fig:defect}), for the chain of
  the field equation (Fig.~\ref{fig:chain}) and for the linear field equation (Fig.~\ref{fig:fieldeq}).
\item \textbf{Test numbers}: the planned tests numbered F6.8g and F6.9 in the previous version are renumbered F6.8i
  and F6.11 (Sec.~\ref{sec:next-tests}), because the laboratory has given these numbers to the young-field stiffness and to
  the vacuum control (F6.9a).
\end{itemize}
