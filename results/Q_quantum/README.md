# Q: The quantum layer

## Objective

Quantum instruments and tests on the causal order: amplitudes over chains instead of non-negative flows, interference,
the quantum measure and the Born rule, Bell correlations in growth, records. Kept apart from the classical phases
K, F0-F8 (decision 49). The first tests are calibrations of quantum instruments on a bare Poisson sprinkling of flat
spacetime; they depend on no open classical pillar. Every test has a null model (the classical, non-negative flow);
a quantum effect counts only where it differs from it.

## Contents

One folder per test, `Q_quantum/<ID>/`, with the records of its full runs, in the same format as the other phases.
The verdicts and their reading are in `docs/quantum/findings.md`; the planned tests in `docs/quantum/planned_tests.csv`.
