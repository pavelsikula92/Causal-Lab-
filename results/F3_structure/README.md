# F3: Formation of structure and matter

## Objective

What matter is in a causal order: flows that carry their four-velocity, attempts at a bundle bound by its own field, locked defects of a carried orientation, and defects (kinks, walls) of a field on all events evolved by the retarded Green function of the order - their identity, inertia, swerves - and the Green function itself.

## Contents

One folder per test, `F3_structure/<ID>/`, with the records of its full runs: `<UTC time>_<part>_seed<seed>_<test hash 8>.json` (parameters, seed, hashes of the test file and of the core, versions, the printed lines and the data) and the same lines as `.txt`. Reduced runs are in `<ID>/quick/` and do not count. Nothing is overwritten.

The verdicts and their reading are in `docs/findings.md`; the list of tests with their latest states in `docs/test_list.md` (`python run.py report`); the records of one test: `python run.py results <ID>`.
