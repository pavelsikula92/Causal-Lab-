# F6: Dynamics of the order

## Objective

How the order itself responds and what action weights it: local growth rules, the phases of the Benincasa-Dowker action, the expected BD action to second order in a static field (the Einstein-Hilbert stiffness of its conformal, mixed and optical sectors, the non-local age terms, the finite-amplitude response), the vacuum control, and the source of the order from a matter action that depends on the order alone - together the field equation of the order.

## Contents

One folder per test, `F6_order_dynamics/<ID>/`, with the records of its full runs: `<UTC time>_<part>_seed<seed>_<test hash 8>.json` (parameters, seed, hashes of the test file and of the core, versions, the printed lines and the data) and the same lines as `.txt`. Reduced runs are in `<ID>/quick/` and do not count. Nothing is overwritten.

The verdicts and their reading are in `docs/findings.md`; the list of tests with their latest states in `docs/test_list.md` (`python run.py report`); the records of one test: `python run.py results <ID>`.
