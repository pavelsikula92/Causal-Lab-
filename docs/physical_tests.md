## 2. Predictions of the discreteness (where the theory says something new)

Every prediction has a fixed **shape** and one free **scale**, the non-locality length ℓ_k.

### P2 — the age term (the most promising physical test)

- **Statement:** a static field that has existed for a time T responds more weakly at the scale L = 1/k by
  δ ≈ 0.10 k³ ℓ_k² T (c = 1). The deviation becomes of order one at L* = (0.1035 ℓ_k² T)^(1/3).
- **Experiment:** searches for deviations from the inverse-square law at short distances: torsion balances (Eöt-Wash
  group, Lee et al. 2020: no deviation down to 52 µm; HUST group), micro-cantilevers and Casimir-shielded experiments
  at the micrometre scale.
- **Why it is striking:** if T is cosmological (the age of the universe) and ℓ_k is at the collider bound
  (~10⁻¹⁹ m), the theory places the deviation at **L* ≈ 50 µm, exactly where torsion balances measure today.** The
  existing result at 52 µm (deviation < 1 %) already gives ℓ_k ≲ 10⁻²⁰ m for cosmological T. Each factor of 5 in L
  improves the bound on ℓ_k by about 11 (ℓ_k ∝ L^(3/2)); an experiment at 10 µm would reach ℓ_k ~ 10⁻²¹ m.
- **What decides it:** the physical meaning of T (age of the field, of the source, or the cosmological past; test
  F8.8, a question of the theory). If T is the age of a laboratory source (hours to years), δ is far below any
  measurement (e.g. ~10⁻¹⁴ at 52 µm for T of one hour and ℓ_k = 10⁻¹⁹ m).
- **A possible discriminating experiment (idea, not designed):** if T is the age of the field configuration, a
  rotating attractor renews its field every period; the measured strength at a fixed distance would then depend on the
  rotation rate. Only meaningful if the reading of T makes the effect large enough.
- **Open before it can be a prediction:** the asymptotic optical age term (F6.8e), the covariance of the sectors
  (F6.8d), the full static propagator (F6.9), the meaning of T (F8.8).

### P1 — the static field of a point mass near ℓ_k

- Shape fixed by the three coefficients A, B, C of the action of the order; only ℓ_k free. Observable content for
  admissible ℓ_k is the age term (P2); the local part is ~10⁻²⁸ at 52 µm, out of reach.
- Experiment: as P2. Needs F8.1 (Φ(r), γ(r) in units of ℓ_k).

### P3 — the static pole of the spin-0 channel

- At L* (with the age term). For cosmological T and ℓ_k = 10⁻¹⁹ m it lies at ~50 µm, the same window as P2. Whether it
  is a standing wave, an instability or the limit of the expansion is open; if it is physical, it is a sharp feature
  at a definite distance, which torsion balances could see.

### P4 — the cosmological constant

- Shared with causal set theory (everpresent Λ, Ahmed, Dodelson, Greene, Sorkin 2004): Λ of the observed order of
  magnitude, fluctuating over cosmic time.
- Observations: supernovae, baryon acoustic oscillations, the cosmic microwave background constrain the time
  dependence of dark energy (the DESI survey reported hints of an evolving dark energy in 2024–2025, to be checked and
  followed). Not yet a prediction of Causal Physics itself (open in the paper).

## 4. Future physical tests (when the theory gives them)

- **The strong field.** The throat, the missing horizon, the shadow larger by 0.91 % and the second post-Newtonian
  coefficients were withdrawn as predictions (decision 13: the non-linear completion is general relativity). If a
  dynamics of the order gives its own strong field, the tests are black-hole shadows (EHT, next-generation EHT),
  ringdown spectra of gravitational waves, neutron-star masses.
- **The density of matter from defects** (docs/cosmogenesis.md, H3): if matter forms as locked defects in the cooling
  early order, the theory must give their number; it must match the observed density of matter (and avoid the
  monopole problem).
- **Galaxies.** The paper makes no claim of a modified low-acceleration regime; the first version had a₀ = cH₀/2π.
  If a mechanism is ever derived, the radial-acceleration relation of galaxies and galaxy clusters are the tests.
