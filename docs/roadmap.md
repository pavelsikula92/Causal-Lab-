## Aim

To test whether gravity and inertia are two manifestations of one dynamics of causal resistance
(*Physics of Causality and GTS*, February 2026; mathematical basis *Causal Physics, Part I*):
R = Σ (I_v/Π_v) Δτ = Σ Z_v Δτ, matter as a persistent structure of high impedance, inertia and gravity as
paths of least cumulative resistance. The laboratory tests the theory and does not rescue it; it must not put
the geometry or the law it looks for into the result.

The chain: **causal structure → transport and resistance → effective geometry → gravitational and inertial behaviour →
independent observable predictions.**

## Three levels of conclusion

| Level | May claim | Does not prove by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mechanism | the mechanism works in the declared model and range | that the mechanisms form a closed theory |
| chain | linked mechanisms together reproduce the predicted behaviour | that the chain corresponds to nature |
| prediction | an independent result predicted in advance succeeded by its criterion | the other consequences of the theory |

The instruments (phase K) have their own category, **calibration**, with no physical conclusion. Phase F8 is a
prediction only when nothing was calibrated on the test data.

## Rules

- The criterion is in the header of the test and does not change after the run. A change of the criterion = a new
  revision of the test (F1.2b) with the reason in its header; the old result stays and holds by the original criterion.
- A verdict holds only after a replication with another seed (`--seed K`).
- The level of the conclusion is mandatory; the output must not claim more than the level allows.
- An instrument is used only in the range of its calibration.
- A new hypothesis may arise after a failure, but it must not change retroactively the criteria by which the old test
  failed.
- Negative results are kept.
