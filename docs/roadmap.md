## Aim

To test whether gravity and inertia are two manifestations of one dynamics of the causal order (*Physics of Causality*,
November 2026; *Physics of Causality and GTS*, February 2026). The laboratory tests the theory and does not rescue
it; it must not put the geometry or the law it looks for into the result.

The chain: **causal structure → transport and response → effective geometry → gravitational and inertial behaviour →
independent observable predictions.**

**The classical programme** (decision 18, 11 Nov) asks one question: how the causal order carries gravity, and what
changes with the dimension (1+1 → 2+1 → 3+1).
- **What a particle is** belongs to the quantum part, which is built on the classical base afterwards.
- **A defect is a test body:** a localised energy that keeps its shape on the order and can be weighed, launched and
  let fall.
- **A field φ on the order is an instrument.** That matter does not arise from the order by itself is not a gap of the
  classical programme.

## Three levels of conclusion

| Level | May claim | Does not prove by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mechanism | the mechanism works in the declared model and range | that the mechanisms form a closed theory |
| chain | linked mechanisms together reproduce the predicted behaviour | that the chain corresponds to nature |
| prediction | an independent result predicted in advance succeeded by its criterion | the other consequences of the theory |

The instruments (phase K) have their own category, **calibration**, with no physical conclusion. Phase F8 is a
prediction only when nothing was calibrated on the test data.

## Rules

**Criteria and cards**
- **Criteria are frozen before the run.** A card is frozen by a decision of the author in docs/author_decisions.md,
  with the md5 of the test file. The criterion is in the header of the test and does not change after the run (R132).
- **Revisions are new tests.** A change of the criterion is a new revision with its own id (F1.2b, F3.16b, F6.8e2) and
  the reason in its header. The old result stays and holds by its original criterion.

**Development and verdicts**
- **Development rows decide nothing.** Tolerances may be set from them (rule of 10 Nov). Data seen in development are
  not counted as confirmation, and the deciding rows are rows not computed before the card.
- **Statistical form of the criteria** (decision 28, the default): the instrument as a significance test against
  predictions computed by frozen code, physics with margins from a bootstrap of the development rows, sign criteria as
  a difference ± 2 SE.
- **Replication.** A verdict holds only after a replication with another seed (`--seed K`). A deterministic
  computation has no seed; there the level checks of the quadrature take the place of the replication.
- **Verdict order:** INSTRUMENT_FAILED → FAILED → PASSED → UNDECIDED.

**Reading and records**
- **Level.** The level of the conclusion is mandatory, and the output must not claim more than the level allows.
- **Instruments.** An instrument is used only in the range of its calibration.
- **New hypotheses.** A new hypothesis may arise after a failure, but it must not change retroactively the criteria by
  which the old test failed.
- **Negative results are kept** in docs/findings.md and planned_tests.csv.
- **IDs.** Every new test gets an id in its phase that is not yet used (check planned_tests.csv), and the card name
  (B1, C0, …) is mapped to it in the table above.
